# Reading List

Papers, books, and datasets referenced in the lecture materials, grouped by week.

## Week 1 — NLP Foundations

- [Sentiment Labelled Sentences dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/331/sentiment%2Blabelled%2Bsentences) (UCI Machine Learning Repository) — used in [Lab sheet 1](../weeks/week-01-nlp-foundations/resources/Lab%20sheet%201.pdf).

## Week 2 — Transformers and Attention

- Vaswani, A. et al. (2017). ["Attention Is All You Need."](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) *NeurIPS 2017.* The paper that introduced the Transformer architecture — full PDF in [week-02 resources](../weeks/week-02-transformers-and-attention/resources/1706.03762v7.pdf).

## Week 3 — LLM Internals and Pre-training

- Alammar, J. & Grootendorst, M. (2024). *Hands-On Large Language Models.* O'Reilly Media. Reference book for the "Looking Inside LLMs" lesson.
- [TinyStories dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories) (Hugging Face) — used in [Lab sheet 3](../weeks/week-03-llm-internals-and-pretraining/resources/Lab%20sheet%203.pdf) to train a tiny Transformer from scratch.

## Week 4 — Prompt Engineering and Fine-Tuning

- [databricks-dolly-15k dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/databricks/databricks-dolly-15k) (Hugging Face) — example instruction/response dataset referenced for Supervised Fine-Tuning.
- [Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct) and [SmolLM2-360M-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-360M-Instruct) (Hugging Face) — small instruction-tuned models suggested in [Lab sheet 4](../weeks/week-04-prompt-engineering-and-fine-tuning/resources/Lab%20sheet%204.pdf) for LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning; `SmolLM2-360M-Instruct` was used in my [Lab 4 notebook](../weeks/week-04-prompt-engineering-and-fine-tuning/lab/lab4_fine_tuning.ipynb).
