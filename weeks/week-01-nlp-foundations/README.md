# Week 1 — NLP Foundations

From classical NLP to the building blocks used by modern generative AI: what NLP is, how raw
text is cleaned and represented numerically, and how neural approaches improved on
traditional methods.

## Key concepts

- **What is NLP** — the field bridging human language and computers (sentiment analysis, NER,
  text classification, speech recognition, chatbots, Q&A)
- **Text preprocessing** — lowercasing, punctuation/URL/emoji removal, contraction expansion
- **Tokenization** — sentence-, word-, character-, and sub-word-level tokenization (and why
  sub-word tokenization is used in BERT/GPT/T5)
- **Stop words, stemming & lemmatization** — when to use each
- **Text representation** — one-hot encoding, Bag-of-Words, N-grams, and their limitations
  (no semantics, no word order, sparse vectors)
- **Word embeddings** — Word2Vec (CBOW/skip-gram), contextual embeddings (BERT), and why
  dense vectors capture semantic similarity
- **Neural NLP architectures** — RNN, LSTM, CNN, and Transformers compared
