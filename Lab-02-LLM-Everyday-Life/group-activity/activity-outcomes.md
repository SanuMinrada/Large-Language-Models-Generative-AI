# Activity Outcomes

**Task (Lab sheet 2, "AI in everyday challenges"):** take a real student problem from a challenge card and use an LLM as a product-development partner. Design the product concept, name, key features, target user and a mock-up, then present it in 2–3 minutes.

**Our challenge:** The Forgetful Student, who leaves home without the things they need for the day.

## Our product: PackWise

A smart packing companion that reads a student's timetable, builds a daily packing list from it, and reminds them before they leave.

- **Target user:** university students with a busy, changing timetable, who often forget things
  like chargers, ID cards, or USB drives before heading out
- **Key features:**
  - Reads the student's timetable
  - Builds a packing list for each day from it
  - Sends a reminder before the student leaves (location-based "leave-zones" like home or the
    lecture hall)
  - Lets the student scan their bag with their phone camera to check what's missing
  - A chat assistant you can ask "what do I need today/tomorrow?"
  - Tracks what the student forgets most often over time

## How we used the LLM

We used the LLM to brainstorm names for the product before picking PackWise, and to help us think
past the obvious "just make a checklist app" idea. The bag-scan feature and the "what you forget
most" stat on the profile screen both came out of that brainstorming. We also used it to help
write the text used in the app mock-up and the presentation slides.
